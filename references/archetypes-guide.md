# Complete Slide Archetypes & Layout Blueprints (Archetypes A–H)

This reference defines the 8 clinical and scientific presentation archetypes supported by `medical-journal-slides`. Every incoming medical paper maps cleanly to one of these blueprints.

---

## Archetype A: Clinical Trial Plenary Deck (CONSORT / ASCO / ESMO Style)
- **Best For**: Phase II/III Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs), drug/device interventions.
- **Default Range**: 8–10 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Bookend Dark)**: Trial title, acronym, authors, journal citation, DOI.
2. **Slide 2 (Clinical Background - Open 2-Col)**: Disease burden, standard-of-care gaps, biological rationale.
3. **Slide 3 (Study Design & Eligibility - Boxed 3-Col Pillars)**: Population ($N$, inclusion), Randomization arms & dosing, Primary/Secondary endpoints.
4. **Slide 4 (Baseline Characteristics - Native Clinical Table)**: Demographics and disease markers across arms (max 6 cols x 8 rows).
5. **Slide 5 (Primary Efficacy Endpoint - Split Hero + 300 DPI Curve)**: `44–48 pt` Bold HR/RR, 95% CI, p-value + cropped Kaplan-Meier plot.
6. **Slide 6 (Subgroup & Secondary Endpoints - Open 2-Col / Forest Plot)**: Cropped Forest plot + secondary outcomes (ORR, OS, QoL).
7. **Slide 7 (Safety & Tolerability - Native Toxicity Table)**: All-grade vs Grade ≥ 3 AEs marked with `[⚠]` and discontinuation rates.
8. **Slide 8 (Terminal Take-Home Outro - Bookend Dark)**: Single 6–12 word practice-changing conclusion + DOI rail.

## Archetype B: Hospital Journal Club & Critical Appraisal (IMRaD + CASP)
- **Best For**: Residency journal clubs, departmental morning reports, EBM critical appraisal.
- **Default Range**: 8–12 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Dark)**: Clinical dilemma + full journal citation.
2. **Slide 2 (Clinical Scenario & PICO - Boxed 3-Col)**: Population, Intervention vs Comparator, Outcomes.
3. **Slide 3 (Methodology & CONSORT - Open 2-Col)**: Randomization, blinding, allocation concealment, ITT vs Per-Protocol.
4. **Slide 4 (Primary Efficacy & NNT - Split Hero)**: Absolute risk reduction (ARR), Relative risk (RR/HR), 95% CI, and NNT.
5. **Slide 5 (Secondary Outcomes & Safety - Table)**: Secondary endpoints and NNH / Grade ≥ 3 adverse events (`[⚠]`).
6. **Slide 6 (Internal Validity Appraisal - Open 2-Col CASP)**: Selection, performance, detection, and attrition biases (`[✓]` / `[⚠]`).
7. **Slide 7 (External Validity & Local Applicability - Open 2-Col)**: Study limitations vs feasibility in local hospital patient population.
8. **Slide 8 (Terminal Clinical Verdict - Dark)**: Clear 1-sentence verdict on whether local institutional practice should change.

## Archetype C: Visual Abstract / Quick Take (NEJM / JAMA Style)
- **Best For**: 1–3 slide executive summaries, committee briefings, rapid research dissemination.
- **Default Range**: 1–3 Slides *(Exempt from the multi-slide 1/3 card quota)*
- **Slide 1 (Visual Abstract Grid)**:
  - **Title Band**: Research finding as an active headline + journal citation kicker.
  - **Body Band (3 Boxed Pillars)**: Pillar 1 = Population ($N$, criteria) | Pillar 2 = Intervention vs Comparator | Pillar 3 = Primary Outcome (`44 pt` Bold metric, HR/RR, 95% CI, p-value).
  - **Footer Band**: One-sentence clinical conclusion + DOI.

---

## Archetype D: Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis (PRISMA / Cochrane)
- **Best For**: Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, umbrella reviews.
- **Default Range**: 8–10 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Dark)**: Review title, PICO objective, PROSPERO registration ID.
2. **Slide 2 (PRISMA Selection Flow - Open + Cropped Flowchart)**: Databases searched, records screened, final included studies ($k$ studies, total $N$).
3. **Slide 3 (Characteristics of Included Trials - Native Table)**: Summary of study designs, populations, interventions, and follow-up.
4. **Slide 4 (Risk of Bias Matrix - Native Table)**: Cochrane RoB-2 / AMSTAR-2 quality domains (`[✓]` Low, `[⚠]` High/Unclear).
5. **Slide 5 (Pooled Primary Outcome - Split Hero + Forest Plot)**: Pooled effect size (OR/RR/SMD), 95% CI, heterogeneity (`I²`, p-value).
6. **Slide 6 (Subgroup, Sensitivity & Publication Bias - Open 2-Col)**: Funnel plot symmetry, Egger test, subgroup moderators.
7. **Slide 7 (GRADE Evidence Profile - Boxed Pillars or Table)**: Certainty of evidence and clinical direction.
8. **Slide 8 (Terminal Take-Home Outro - Dark)**: Bottom-line evidence synthesis statement.

## Archetype E: Narrative, Mechanistic, or Mini Review (SANRA Style)
- **Best For**: Mechanistic reviews, translational medicine overviews, clinical trial synthesis reviews (e.g., *Frontiers*, *Nature Reviews*, *Nutrients*).
- **Default Range**: 8–10 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Bookend Dark)**: Review title, authors, journal citation, DOI.
2. **Slide 2 (Clinical Burden & Conceptual Framework - Open 2-Col + Cropped Graphical Abstract)**: Epidemiology, unmet clinical need, and core physiological axis.
3. **Slide 3 (Core Biological & Molecular Pathways - Split 2-Col + Cropped Pathway Figure)**: Key signaling routes (e.g., neural/vagal, endocrine/HPA, immune, metabolites) with 300 DPI cropped diagram.
4. **Slide 4 (Clinical Evidence Synthesis I: Healthy / Preventive Cohorts - Native Table)**: Trial design, strain/agent, dose/duration, and key physiological/cognitive findings.
5. **Slide 5 (Clinical Evidence Synthesis II: Symptomatic / Clinical Cohorts - Native Table)**: RCTs in stressed, anxious, depressed, or neurodivergent patients with `[✓]` / `[⚠]` outcome markers.
6. **Slide 6 (Strain- or Agent-Specific Molecular Mechanisms - Boxed 3-Col Pillars)**: Specific bioactive metabolites (e.g., ILA/AhR, SCFA/isovalerate, GABA/serotonin) and biomarker predictors.
7. **Slide 7 (Moderators of Clinical Efficacy & Translational Challenges - Open 2-Col)**: Dose (CFU), delivery matrix (micro-encapsulation), duration (8–12 wks), baseline symptom severity, and lifestyle synergy.
8. **Slide 8 (Future Perspectives & Precision Medicine Gaps - Open 2-Col)**: Multi-omics integration, standardized RCT protocols, and adjunctive therapy potential.
9. **Slide 9 (Terminal Take-Home Outro - Bookend Dark)**: Single 6–12 word overarching clinical thesis + DOI rail.

## Archetype F: Observational Cohort / Case-Control Study (STROBE Style)
- **Best For**: Prospective/retrospective cohorts, registry studies, case-control, cross-sectional studies.
- **Default Range**: 8–10 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Dark)** → **Slide 2 (Background & Aim)** → **Slide 3 (Cohort Selection & Exposure Definition - Boxed 3-Col)** → **Slide 4 (Baseline Characteristics by Exposure - Table)** → **Slide 5 (Primary Multivariable Adjusted Outcomes - Chart/Table)** → **Slide 6 (Subgroup & Dose-Response Analyses)** → **Slide 7 (Confounding, Bias & STROBE Limitations)** → **Slide 8 (Terminal Take-Home Outro - Dark)**.

---

## Archetype G: Clinical Practice Guideline / Consensus Statement (AGREE II / GRADE)
- **Best For**: Society guidelines (ESC, AHA, ASCO, IDSA, WHO), consensus statements.
- **Default Range**: 8–10 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Dark)** → **Slide 2 (Why New Guidelines Were Needed)** → **Slide 3 (Diagnostic & Risk Stratification Algorithm)** → **Slide 4 (First-Line Therapeutic Recommendations - Table with Class & Level of Evidence)** → **Slide 5 (Second-Line & Refractory Management)** → **Slide 6 (Special Populations & Comorbidities)** → **Slide 7 (Safety Monitoring & Contraindications `[⚠]`)** → **Slide 8 (Terminal Ten Commandments / Key Takeaway - Dark)**.

---

## Archetype H: Clinical Case Report & Diagnostic Workup (CARE Style)
- **Best For**: Rare clinical presentations, diagnostic dilemmas, novel adverse events, morbidity & mortality (M&M) rounds.
- **Default Range**: 6–8 Slides
1. **Slide 1 (Cover - Dark)** → **Slide 2 (Chief Complaint, History & Timeline - Boxed 3-Col)** → **Slide 3 (Physical Exam & Initial Laboratory Workup - Table)** → **Slide 4 (Imaging, Histopathology & Differential Diagnosis - Cropped 300 DPI Figures)** → **Slide 5 (Definitive Diagnosis, Treatment & Clinical Course)** → **Slide 6 (Literature Context & Pathophysiology)** → **Slide 7 (Terminal Clinical Pearls Outro - Dark)**.
